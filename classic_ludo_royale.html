Official Rules & Power Tiles

• Exiting the Yard: Roll a 6 to deploy a 3D pawn onto your colored starting star square.

• Bonus Rolls: Rolling a 6, capturing an opponent's pawn, reaching the center finish triangle, or landing on a 🎲 +1 Die Tile awards an immediate bonus roll! Three consecutive 6s forfeit your turn.

• Safe Zones & Captures: All 8 Star Squares (4 colored starts + 4 slate stars) and 🚨 Beacon Tiles protect pawns from capture. Landing on a single or stacked opponent on any other square sends them zipping back to their yard!

• Rocket Tiles (🚀): When Power Tiles are ON, landing on a Rocket boosts your pawn +2 steps forward along the main track.
